# Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar: City Profile & Smart Operations

## City Overview: Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (CSN)

### Historical Background

**Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar**, formerly known as **Aurangabad**, is a historic city in the **Marathwada region of Maharashtra**. The city was renamed in 2022 to honor **Chhatrapati Sambhaji**, the Marathi warrior king and son of Shivaji.

### Geographic & Demographic Profile

| Metric | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Location** | Marathwada region, Maharashtra, India |
| **Population** | 1.1+ million (Census 2021) |
| **Area** | ~300 sq km (city proper) |
| **Elevation** | ~570 meters above sea level |
| **Climate** | Semi-arid, Temperature Range: 10°C (Winter) - 42°C (Summer) |
| **Official Language** | Marathi, Hindi (secondary) |
| **Tourist Status** | Tourism Capital of Maharashtra |

## Tourism & Cultural Significance

### UNESCO World Heritage Sites

**Ajanta Caves** (30 km from city center)
- 30 Buddhist rock-cut caves carved into cliffs
- Dating back to 2nd century BCE - 5th century CE
- Famous for ancient murals and sculptures
- Estimated 500,000+ visitors annually

**Ellora Caves** (29 km from city center)
- 34 caves representing Buddhist, Hindu, and Jain traditions
- 5th - 10th century CE architecture
- UNESCO recognition since 1983
- Multi-religious significance (rare in archaeology)

### Historic Landmarks

**Daulatabad Fort (Devgiri Fort)**
- Historic 14th-century fortress overlooking the city
- Strategic location on elevated terrain
- Important military and administrative center
- Tourist attraction with climbing pathways

**Bibi Ka Maqbara**
- Mausoleum built by Aurangzeb for his wife Rabia-ud-Daurani
- Often called "Mini Taj Mahal" (pale counterpart)
- 17th-century Indo-Islamic architecture
- Popular photography spot

**Grishneshwar Temple**
- Ancient Hindu temple, one of 12 sacred Jyotirlingas
- Dedicated to Lord Shiva
- Major pilgrimage destination
- 500+ years old

**52 Historic Gates (Dar Gates)**
- Historic city fortification system
- Named gates include: Bhadkal Gate, Delhi Gate, Paithan Gate
- Medieval defensive architecture
- Cultural heritage preservation ongoing
