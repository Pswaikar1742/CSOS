CHHATRAPATI SAMBHAJINAGAR CITY POLICE & TRAFFIC DEPARTMENT
POLICE SURVEILLANCE & RESPONSE STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

Issued For: Integrated Command & Control Center (ICCC)
System: ASCDCL Smart City Surveillance Grid
Status: Smart City Pilot / Demo SOP


1. PURPOSE

This document defines the operational procedures for real-time crime detection, threat assessment, surveillance validation, and police response within the Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar city limits using the ASCDCL (Aurangabad Smart City Development Corporation Ltd.) CCTV network.


2. CCTV JURISDICTION & SURVEILLANCE GRID

All surveillance cameras are operated under ASCDCL and routed to the ICCC.

Primary monitored zones include:
- Kranti Chowk
- Aurangpura Market
- CIDCO N-6
- Nirala Bazar
- Garkheda
- Waluj MIDC

All vehicle registrations in city jurisdiction fall under MH-20 and MH-20-XX series.


3. THREAT CLASSIFICATION LEVELS

Level 1 Threat:
- Unarmed verbal or physical altercation
- Public disturbance without visible weapons
- Group aggression or crowd escalation

Level 2 Threat:
- Presence of sharp object or handheld weapon
- Knife, iron rod, bat, stick, or blunt object detected
- Potential for immediate bodily harm

Level 3 Threat:
- Firearm detected
- Pistol, revolver, country-made weapon
- Active shooter risk or imminent lethal threat


4. NEURAL DETECTION & VERIFICATION PROTOCOL

1. AI vision system continuously scans CCTV feeds.
2. A threat is considered valid only if detected with confidence greater than 85%.
3. The detected object must persist for a minimum of 15 consecutive frames.
4. Only verified detections are forwarded to Police Command to avoid false alarms.


5. RESPONSE UNITS & DISPATCH AUTHORITY

Available Units:
- Cheetah Units: Two-wheeler rapid response bike patrols
- PCR Vans: Four-wheeler emergency response vehicles
- Traffic Interceptors: Junction interception units

Dispatch Authority:
- ICCC Central Police Command Desk


6. RESPONSE RULES BY THREAT LEVEL

Level 1:
- Notify nearest police station
- Live CCTV monitoring continues
- No immediate field dispatch unless escalated

Level 2:
- Immediate dispatch of nearest Cheetah Unit
- Inform Station House Officer (SHO)
- PCR Van placed on standby

Level 3:
- Immediate dispatch of minimum two units (Cheetah + PCR Van)
- Senior Inspector and ACP informed
- Live feed locked for evidence review
- Area cordoning initiated


7. ANPR & VEHICLE INTERCEPTION PROTOCOL

1. All detected number plates are scanned via ANPR.
2. Plates are checked against Police and RTO blacklist databases.
3. If a vehicle is marked BLACKLISTED:
   - ANPR grid is locked
   - Interception is initiated at the next major junction
   - Priority junctions include Kranti Chowk and CIDCO traffic signals
4. MH-20 series plates receive priority matching.


8. EVIDENCE PRESERVATION RULES

1. For all Level 2 and Level 3 incidents:
   - System must capture at least 3 image frames of the weapon.
2. Each frame must contain:
   - Timestamp
   - Camera ID
   - Location or ward identifier
3. Metadata is stored in an immutable police audit log.
4. Raw video frames are retained only as per legal and investigation requirements.


9. OFFICER SAFETY & ENGAGEMENT RULES

- Solo engagement is prohibited for Level 2 and Level 3 threats.
- Field units must maintain safe distance until backup arrives.
- ICCC maintains continuous live monitoring until incident closure.


10. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)

Level 1 Response Time: Within 10 minutes  
Level 2 Response Time: Within 5 minutes  
Level 3 Response Time: Immediate dispatch within 2 minutes  


11. EXAMPLE OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVE

"Upon detection of a Level 2 threat in the Aurangpura market area, the central command must alert the nearest Cheetah bike patrol and lock the ANPR grid for MH-20 series vehicles."
